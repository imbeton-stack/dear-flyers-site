Dear Passengers support

Privacy policy

Effective October 17, 2026. This policy applies to Dear Passengers: Cabin Crew for Android and Dear Flyers: Passenger Rush for iOS, developed and operated by Sergei Markhel. Contact: Beton8@yandex.ru.

Game and local storage

No account or login is required. Campaign progress, stars, preferences and language choices are stored on your device. The game has no player chat, real multiplayer, in-app purchases or subscriptions in this version. Gameplay can be used offline.

Analytics

We use GameAnalytics to understand game usage and improve the game. Its SDK processes a persistent installation identifier, session information, app/device information and gameplay events such as level starts, results, stars and active playing time. We do not supply your name or email as analytics identifiers. Advertising-ID tracking for GameAnalytics and automatic exception/log submission are disabled in the game's integration. Events recorded offline may be sent when a connection becomes available. See GameAnalytics' privacy policy for its processing and retention practices.

Advertising and consent

The game integrates Appodeal advertising, the direct AppLovin adapter and Stack Consent Manager. Full-screen ads may appear after a flight result. Advertising services may process IP addresses, app/device information, advertising identifiers when permitted, consent choices and ad interaction data for ad delivery, measurement, fraud prevention and, where permitted, personalization. Development and internal TestFlight builds use test advertising; test mode should not be understood as disabling all SDK data processing.

Consent forms can depend on your region and device settings. On Android, advertising identifier controls are available in your device settings. Apple's tracking permission applies on iOS. You can decline tracking in the iOS permission prompt and change the app's tracking permission in iOS Settings. Privacy Options in the game's Settings provide the consent choices available for your region. The game remains playable when ads or a network connection are unavailable. See the Appodeal privacy policy and AppLovin privacy policy.

Sharing, transfers and retention

Data is processed by the services above for the purposes described, and may be processed outside your country subject to their applicable safeguards and policies. Local progress remains until removed through app/device storage controls or uninstallation. SDKs manage their own identifiers, offline queues and retention. We do not promise that uninstalling removes information already received by these services.

Your choices and requests

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or restrict processing of personal data, withdraw consent or object to processing. Contact us at the address above for assistance; do not send advertising identifiers or other sensitive information until needed to handle a request. You may also contact the relevant service using its policy. Offline gameplay does not require advertising or analytics connectivity.

Request deletion of your data

Dear Passengers: Cabin Crew is published by Beton (Sergei Markhel). No player account is created.

  1. Email Beton8@yandex.ru with the subject “Dear Passengers data deletion”.
  2. Say whether you want to delete local game progress, analytics records, advertising data or support correspondence. Include your platform, device model and approximate dates of play so the relevant records or service can be identified. Do not send passwords. An installation or advertising identifier may be needed to locate SDK records; we will explain what is needed.
  3. For local progress, use Android app storage controls or uninstall the game. This removes progress and settings on that device. It does not by itself delete data previously received by SDK providers.

Identifiable analytics events and installation identifiers are handled under GameAnalytics’ retention and deletion policy. Advertising identifiers, ad interactions and associated technical data are handled under the Appodeal and AppLovin policies linked above. AppLovin’s privacy tools also allow you to request deletion directly. AppLovin states that advertising-service information is typically retained for up to two years, often less for device data, or until deletion is requested, with exceptions for legal obligations and dispute resolution. Each provider controls its processing and deletion timetable. We can assist with routing your request; records must first be identifiable. Anonymous aggregate statistics and information required for legal obligations may be retained. We do not claim that all SDK data is automatically deleted within 90 days.

Support and children

If you email support, we receive your address and the information you choose to send and use it to resolve your request. The game does not ask players to provide their name, contact details or date of birth. Parents or guardians who believe a child has supplied personal information can contact us for assistance.

Updates

We may update this policy when the game or its services change. The effective date above identifies this version.
