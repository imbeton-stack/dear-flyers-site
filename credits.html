Game support

Credits and attribution

Dear Flyers: Passenger Rush (Android and iOS), operated by Sergei Markhel.

Game code, procedural geometry, animation, opening sequence and deterministic music/foley were authored for this project. Menu art and item illustrations were generated with OpenAI image generation. Selected 3D models were generated through Tripo and processed for this game.

Offline synthetic speech

The game distributes prerecorded voice clips, not speech engines or voice models. The following source notices identify the voice authors, datasets and licenses. Voice clips were synthesized, edited and converted to WAV for short in-game cues. CC BY notices and source links are reproduced below. Model training and engine licenses do not imply that an online AI service runs in the game.

Attribution.txt
Dear Flyers offline synthetic speech

Original RU/EN: existing Windows SAPI recordings, unchanged.

Piper neural voices: model cards in this folder identify their datasets and licenses. The Italian Paola dataset declares CC0: https://huggingface.co/datasets/paolapersico1/Voice-Dataset-Italian
Piper authoring: https://github.com/OHF-Voice/piper1-gpl

Kokoro-82M v1.0, hexgrad, Apache-2.0: https://huggingface.co/hexgrad/Kokoro-82M . Hindi hf_alpha; Chinese zf_xiaobei. ONNX authoring conversion: https://github.com/thewh1teagle/kokoro-onnx . Chinese script variants share a Mandarin voice.

Japanese: Open JTalk / HTS Voice Mei, MMDAgent Project Team, Nagoya Institute of Technology, Department of Computer Science, copyright 2009-2013. CC-BY-3.0; see OpenJTalk-Mei.txt. Synthesized recordings are derived from this voice.

Turkish, Indonesian and Korean: eSpeak NG formant synthesis, https://github.com/espeak-ng/espeak-ng . These voices are more mechanical than the neural voices.

Only prerecorded WAVs are distributed in the game; no synthesis engine or model is bundled. No paid synthesis service was used. New recordings have not been certified by native listeners.
